# Categories

Top: wrap of category **pills/chips** (name, product count). Small **Add** next to the “Categories” label. Inline rename on the chip; selection highlights the border.

Bottom: for the selected category, job **cards as toggles** (not a combo box). Multiple on = **union** filter of products in this category assigned to those jobs. None on = no job filter. **Unassigned** toggle when that group exists. “All jobs” products match any selected real job.

Summary line: item count and total cost (GBP) of visible products. Product list for the category with totals.

Compact: category wrap still on top; job toggles then product list **stack**.

Do not use this page as the place to add supplier URLs; that is Products.
